Privacy Policy

Draft for testing (2026-09-27-draft-4). This policy will be reviewed by a lawyer before Wishtide launches.

Who we are

Wishtide ("we") runs the Wishtide app and wishtide.app. Wishtide is being set up as a California company. Questions about privacy: wishtide.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your email address (to send sign-in codes). What you choose to share: your dream, its story, cost, items, location, and optionally your first name and age. Your activity: gifts (amount, optional message, and whether they are anonymous), votes, taps on the shoulder, reports, whether you tapped "I'm interested" in the Wishmaker Club, and when you accepted the Terms. Pass the Tide: what your dream meant to you (only if you choose to show it), notes of encouragement you write (and the first name you sign them with, if any), and whether you have passed the tide. Your ID-check result (verified, pending or failed). For group dreams (3 or more people): the provider quote you add, and the email address of the second adult or staff member you name, plus the invite code or confirmation link we send them and whether they confirmed. If you join the website waiting list: your email, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address used only to stop spam.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your ID card or selfie: our verification partner, Stripe, handles those and tells us only the result. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

What others can see

A live dream is public: the dream, its story, location, cost, progress, and the first name and age you chose to show. Gifts show the giver's name unless they give anonymously. The Wishmaker Wall shows wishmakers' names and how many dreams they helped. Your email is never shown. A live dream also shows whether it is a heartfelt dream or an Open Wish, what the dream meant to the dreamer if they chose to show it, and how many people whose own dream came true helped it (no names). A note of encouragement is seen only by its writer, the dreamer it was sent to (after our check) and Wishtide's admins. The Wishmaker Wall and My dreams can show a "Passed the tide" badge. Group-check details (quote, emails, codes) are never public: only the organizer and Wishtide's admins see them.

How we use your information

To run Wishtide: sign you in, show and review dreams, record gifts and votes, keep the community safe, and send service emails. For group dreams, we use the email address you give us only to send that person one invite code (family) or one confirmation link (organization), and at most 3 times. Dream text is checked by an AI reviewer (Claude, by Anthropic) against Wishtide's rules; it may approve a dream, send it to a person, or decline it. If you think a decision is wrong, email us and a person will look again. Our team also uses a daily report to spot fraud (for example, copied stories). If you ask the in-app help assistant a question, we keep the question (not your account details) for 90 days to improve our answers. Provider options: after you share a dream, an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) searches public websites for up to 3 well-known providers or stores and an estimated price. It uses your dream's text and the city and state you shared; it never contacts providers. Only you and Wishtide's admins see the options.

Beta activity log

While Wishtide is in beta, when you're signed in the app records which screens you open and which main buttons you tap (for example "Give", "Share a dream" or "Share this dream"), with the time and a few simple details such as the kind of gift or how many form fields were filled. We never record what you type, your email or your location. Only Wishtide's admins can see this log, we use it only to improve the app, and each entry is deleted automatically after 180 days (or when you delete your account).

Companies that help us

Supabase (database and sign-in, USA), Stripe (identity checks, and payments later), Resend (emails), Anthropic (AI review of dream text, and web search for provider options), Expo (app updates), and GitHub (hosts the website). They process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we close your open dreams, remove your name and age from past dreams, delete your profile, votes, taps, reports and group-check details (quotes, emails and codes), notes of encouragement you wrote or received, and what your dream meant to you, and ask Stripe to erase your ID-check data. We keep a record of past gifts without your name, because stores and wishmakers rely on it. Backups are cleared on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account at any time in the app: My dreams, then "Delete my account". You can also email us to see, correct or delete your information. California residents have these rights under California law, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

Children

Wishtide is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.

Privacy Policy

Draft for testing (2026-09-27-draft-1). This policy will be reviewed by a lawyer before Wishtide launches.

Who we are

Wishtide ("we") runs the Wishtide app and wishtide.app. Wishtide is being set up as a California company. Questions about privacy: wishtide.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your email address (to send sign-in codes). What you choose to share: your dream, its story, cost, items, location, and optionally your first name and age. Your activity: gifts (amount, optional message, and whether they are anonymous), votes, taps on the shoulder, reports, whether you tapped "I'm interested" in the Wishmaker Club, and when you accepted the Terms. Your ID-check result (verified, pending or failed). If you join the website waiting list: your email, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address used only to stop spam.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your ID card or selfie: our verification partner, Stripe, handles those and tells us only the result. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

What others can see

A live dream is public: the dream, its story, location, cost, progress, and the first name and age you chose to show. Gifts show the giver's name unless they give anonymously. The Wishmaker Wall shows wishmakers' names and how many dreams they helped. Your email is never shown.

How we use your information

To run Wishtide: sign you in, show and review dreams, record gifts and votes, keep the community safe, and send service emails. Dream text is checked by an AI reviewer (Claude, by Anthropic) against Wishtide's rules; it may approve a dream, send it to a person, or decline it. If you think a decision is wrong, email us and a person will look again. Our team also uses a daily report to spot fraud (for example, copied stories).

Companies that help us

Supabase (database and sign-in, USA), Stripe (identity checks, and payments later), Resend (emails), Anthropic (AI review of dream text), Expo (app updates), and GitHub (hosts the website). They process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we close your open dreams, remove your name and age from past dreams, delete your profile, votes, taps and reports, and ask Stripe to erase your ID-check data. We keep a record of past gifts without your name, because stores and wishmakers rely on it. Backups are cleared on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account at any time in the app: My dreams, then "Delete my account". You can also email us to see, correct or delete your information. California residents have these rights under California law, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

Children

Wishtide is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.

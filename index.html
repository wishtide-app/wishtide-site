How it works

Small gifts, one big moment

Wishtide is for dreams that bring lasting joy or a better life: a first flight to meet a grandchild, a washing machine for a grandmother, hearing aids, a little farm. Not luxury things.

1

Share a dream

Finish the sentence "My dream is to…" and tell why it matters. Every dream is reviewed, and every dreamer verifies their ID.

2

Wishmakers make it real

Give the whole dream, chip in with others, or buy the item straight from the store. Companies can match gifts.

3

See the joy

We pay the store, airline or school directly. The dreamer shares a photo or video when the dream comes true.
